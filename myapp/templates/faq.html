Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our data analysis platform. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact our support team.

Account & Registration

How do I create an account?
Click on the "Register" button in the top navigation bar. Fill in your name, email address, and create a password. You'll receive a confirmation and can immediately start using our platform.
Can I have multiple accounts with the same email?
No, each email address can only be associated with one account. This ensures data security and proper user management.
How do I reset my password?
On the login page, click "Forgot Password" and enter your email address. You'll receive instructions to reset your password via email.

File Upload & Management

What file formats are supported?
We currently support CSV (Comma Separated Values) files only. Ensure your file has a .csv extension and follows standard CSV formatting.
Is there a file size limit?
Yes, the maximum file size is 10MB. This ensures optimal performance for all users. For larger files, consider splitting them into smaller chunks.
How many files can I upload?
Each user can store only one CSV file at a time. When you upload a new file, it will replace the previous one. This keeps your workspace organized and ensures optimal system performance.
How do I delete my uploaded file?
Go to your Dashboard, and in the CSV File Management section, click the "Delete CSV" button. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Data Analysis & ML

What machine learning models are available?
Our platform includes pre-trained models for classification, regression, and clustering tasks. The specific model used depends on your data characteristics and the type of analysis you select.
How do I interpret the analysis results?
Results are presented with clear visualizations and statistical summaries. We provide explanations for each metric and visualization to help you understand the insights.
Can I export my analysis results?
Yes, you can download visualizations as PNG images and analysis reports as PDF documents from the analysis results page.

Security & Privacy

Is my data secure?
Absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption for data transmission and storage. Your data is private to your account and is never shared with third parties.
How long is my data stored?
Your data is stored as long as your account is active. If you delete your account or specific files, they are permanently removed from our servers.
Who can access my uploaded data?
Only you can access your uploaded data through your authenticated account. Our team cannot view individual user data unless explicitly granted permission for support purposes.
